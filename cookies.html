Cookie Policy

Effective date: June 29, 2026  ·  Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how AiOne LLC, the company behind Meraqi ("Meraqi," "we," "us," or "our"), uses cookies and similar technologies on our websites and the Meraqi platform (the "Service"). It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function, remember your preferences, and understand how the site is used. We also use related technologies such as local storage and pixels; we refer to all of these as "cookies" here.

2. Categories we use

Our public websites (aionellc.com and meraqi.ai) set no analytics or advertising cookies. We measure page views with a website analytics service that sets no cookies and does not track individuals. Strictly necessary cookies are set only when you sign in to the Meraqi app.

3. Your choices

Managing consent. We do not show a cookie banner on our public websites because they set no non-essential cookies. In the Meraqi app, we ask for your choice on optional product analytics once, when you create your account, with equally easy options to accept or decline. Your answer is stored with your account, applies on every device you sign in from, and can be changed at any time in your account settings. If we ever add non-essential cookies to our websites, we will ask for your consent before setting them.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may break parts of the Service. Many browsers support a "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal; we treat a recognized Global Privacy Control signal as a decline of optional cookies.

4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies may be set by service providers acting on our behalf (for example, analytics or payment providers). Their use of information is governed by their own policies. We do not permit third parties to use cookies to access text-messaging opt-in data or consent.

5. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new effective date.
